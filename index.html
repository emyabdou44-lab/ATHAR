The environment

The environment is defined as everything that surrounds a human being and affects their ability to live on Earth; this encompasses air, water, plants, animals, and much more. Boring defines a person's environment as the sum of stimuli received from birth until death. Broadly speaking, this means the environment includes all manner of forces, influences, and conditions—whether physical, intellectual, economic, political, social, ethical, or emotional. According to Douglas and Holland, the term "environment" is used to describe all external forces, conditions, and influences that impact the nature of life, behavior, growth, and development of living organisms..
